My name is Austin. I'm a cybersecurity analyst.

Most of what I publish here comes out of curiosity: I'll read about an attack technique
or a cloud service, decide I don't really understand it, and then go build it in a lab
until I do. Writing the process or getting hands-on is how I make things stick, plus I can
showcase what I'm interested in!

## What I focus on

- **Homelabs.** I like building things that bring a little
  value to my life. How can I see my local train schedule?
  How can I block ads on my home network?
- **Cloud related things.** I passed the Microsoft Azure Administrator Associate (AZ-104) after spending
  a summer on it, and I wrote up [the full study path]({{< ref "/blog/cloud-certification-microsoft-azure-associate-az-104" >}}).
- **Automation.** Mostly Python
- **Conferences.** I love to learn from people smarter than me
especially when I get to learn something cool that I can try out
