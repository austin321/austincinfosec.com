My name is Austin. I'm a student at **SUNY Buffalo** in the Information Technology &
Management program, with a minor in **Cybersecurity**.

Most of what I publish here comes out of curiosity: I'll read about an attack technique
or a cloud service, decide I don't really understand it, and then go build it in a lab
until I do. Writing the process down is how I make sure the understanding sticks — and
if it helps someone else studying the same thing, even better.

## What I focus on

- **Offensive fundamentals, defensive mindset.** I like building things like backdoors
  and ransomware in disposable VMs, then turning around and asking what a SOC analyst
  would actually see in the logs.
- **Cloud.** I passed the Microsoft Azure Administrator Associate (AZ-104) after spending
  a summer on it, and I wrote up [the full study path]({{< ref "/blog/cloud-certification-microsoft-azure-associate-az-104" >}}).
- **Automation.** Mostly Python — scripting the boring parts until they run themselves.
